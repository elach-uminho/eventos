About

«Terra minha pela Memória e pelo Amor»
Tomaz de Figueiredo: 125 years since his birth | 80 years of A Toca do Lobo

International Conference

Dates: 20–21 May 2027

Venue: Casa das Artes, Arcos de Valdevez (Portugal)

On the occasion of the 125th anniversary of the birth of Tomaz de Figueiredo and the 80th anniversary of the publication of A Toca do Lobo, this conference invites a critical reflection on the work of the writer and his place in twentieth-century Portuguese literature.

Author of a diverse body of work that includes novels, novellas and short stories, as well as poetry, drama, chronicles and memoirs, Tomaz de Figueiredo developed a style of writing deeply shaped by memory, by place, and by the relationship between the individual and the social and historical world. A Toca do Lobo, published in 1946, is a particularly significant moment in this output and, at the same time, the moment of his greatest visibility in the literary polysystem of the Estado Novo (Eça de Queiroz Prize). Looking beyond this novel, which was in fact the beginning of a larger epic project only partially carried out, the conference aims to promote a comprehensive reading of the author's writing and thought.

In this context, it is equally important to question the place of Tomaz de Figueiredo in the history and canon of Portuguese literature. The marginalisation of his work raises questions about the processes of selection, legitimation, transmission and forgetting that take part in the construction of the literary canon, as well as about the need to revisit authors and works left out of the dominant narratives of literary historiography.

Thematic Strands

The conference welcomes interdisciplinary and comparative proposals that contribute to new readings of Tomaz de Figueiredo and to a broader reflection on twentieth-century Portuguese literature, its canon and its margins.

Among other possibilities, proposals addressing the following topics are welcome:

1. Tomaz de Figueiredo and the canon of Portuguese literature

  • processes of canonisation, marginalisation and forgetting;
  • literary historiography and the construction of the canon;
  • critical reassessment of authors and works outside the dominant narratives;

2. A Toca do Lobo and the work of Tomaz de Figueiredo

  • new readings of A Toca do Lobo;
  • relations between A Toca do Lobo and the author's other works;
  • reception, circulation and critical fortune of the work.

3. Literature, territory, memory and identity

  • the Alto Minho and the literary representation of territory;
  • landscape, place and belonging;
  • individual, family and collective memory;

4. Nature, landscape and the rural world

  • representations of nature and landscape;
  • rurality, tradition and social transformation;
  • relations between literature and ecology;

5. Literature, history and ideology

  • Tomaz de Figueiredo and the political and cultural context of the Estado Novo;
  • literature, power and resistance;
  • tradition, modernity and social conflict;

6. Aesthetics, language and literary forms

  • style, language and narrative construction;
  • irony, satire, the grotesque and other strategies of representation;
  • intertextual and interartistic dialogues.

7. Tomaz de Figueiredo in dialogue

  • comparative perspectives with authors, movements and traditions;
  • dialogues with literatures and cultures from other geographies;
  • Tomaz de Figueiredo and the intellectual and literary networks of his time.

More than a celebration, this conference aims to be a space for debate and critical rereading, taking the work of Tomaz de Figueiredo as a starting point to rethink Portuguese literature, its canons and its margins.

Call for Papers — Submission of proposals

Researchers, lecturers, postgraduate students and other interested parties are invited to submit proposals for papers (20 minutes) related to the thematic strands of the conference.

Proposals should include:

  • title of the paper;
  • abstract, of around 200 words;
  • name of the author;
  • institutional affiliation;
  • biographical note, of around 100 words;
  • e-mail address.

Proposals should be sent to

with the subject line Call for Papers – Tomaz de Figueiredo
